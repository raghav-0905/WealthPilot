# SEBI Circular: HNI Advisory & Alternative Investment Regulations

**Circular Ref:** SEBI/HO/IMD/DF2/CIR/P/2026/042  
**Date:** March 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Guidelines on Concentrated Equity Exposure, Lock-in Compliance in AIFs, and RIA Suitability Framework

### 1. Promoter Concentration & Insider Trading Restrictions
- Wealth advisors handling promoter or key managerial personnel (KMP) portfolios must verify trading window compliance under SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations.
- Bulk/block exits exceeding 1% of total paid-up share capital require advance disclosure to stock exchanges and cannot be structured through open-market fragmented orders without prior intimations.

### 2. Alternative Investment Funds (Cat I & II) Minimum Investment & Lock-in
- Under SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, minimum ticket size for Category II AIFs remains ₹1.00 Crore for non-accredited investors (₹25 Lakhs for accredited investors).
- Units of Category II AIFs are subject to mandatory tenure lock-in as specified in Scheme Information Document (typically 3–5 years).
- Premature liquidation or redemption requests cannot be honored prior to scheme maturity. Any portfolio rebalancing strategy assuming early liquidity from Category II AIF holdings is non-viable and legally barred.

### 3. Investment Adviser Suitability Requirements
- RIAs must explicitly verify that client liquidity requirements over a 12 to 36 month horizon do not rely on locked-in alternative asset classes.
