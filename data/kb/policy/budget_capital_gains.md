# Ministry of Finance: Capital Gains & Surcharge Regime Summary

**Reference:** Finance Act 2024 / FY 2024-25 onwards  
**Applicability:** HNI Portfolio Taxation in India

### 1. Capital Gains Tax Rates
- **Listed Equity & Equity-Oriented Mutual Funds (Holding > 12 Months - LTCG):**
  - Taxed at 12.5% under Section 112A.
  - Basic exemption threshold: ₹1,25,000 per financial year on aggregate LTCG.
  - Surcharge capped at 15% for income covered under Section 112A.
- **Listed Equity (Holding <= 12 Months - STCG):**
  - Taxed at 20% under Section 111A.
  - Surcharge capped at 15%.
- **Unlisted Equity:**
  - Holding period for LTCG: 24 months.
  - Taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefit.
- **Commercial Real Estate (Holding > 24 Months):**
  - Taxed at 12.5% without indexation (or optional grandfathering for assets acquired before July 2024).

### 2. Surcharge Slabs for High-Net-Worth Individuals
- Taxable income ₹50 Lakh to ₹1 Crore: 10% surcharge
- Taxable income ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore: 15% surcharge
- Taxable income ₹2 Crore to ₹5 Crore: 25% surcharge (capped at 15% for 111A & 112A capital gains)
- Health & Education Cess: 4% applicable on (Tax + Surcharge) across all brackets.

### 3. Tax Loss Harvesting Rules (Section 70 & 71)
- Long Term Capital Loss (LTCL) can **only** be set off against Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG).
- Short Term Capital Loss (STCL) can be set off against **both** Short Term Capital Gains and Long Term Capital Gains.
- Unabsorbed capital losses can be carried forward up to 8 assessment years, provided the return of income is filed within the due date under Section 139(1).
- Staging large gains across financial years (e.g., splitting a ₹4 Cr sale across FY1 and FY2) allows dual utilization of basic exemption thresholds and prevents liquidity crunch.
