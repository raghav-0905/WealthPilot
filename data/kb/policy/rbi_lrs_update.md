# RBI Master Direction: Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) & Tax Collected at Source (TCS)

**Direction Ref:** RBI/FED/2026-27/11  
**Category:** Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) Guidelines  
**Subject:** Annual LRS Limit, Permissible Overseas Investments, and TCS Collection Framework

### 1. Annual Permissible Limit
- Under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident individuals are permitted to remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year (April–March) for permissible capital and current account transactions.
- Permissible capital account transactions include acquisition of foreign listed shares, ETFs, debt instruments, and offshore property.

### 2. Tax Collected at Source (TCS) Slabs
- For investments in foreign stocks / mutual funds / ETFs via LRS:
  - Remittances up to ₹7,00,000 per FY: Nil TCS.
  - Remittances exceeding ₹7,00,000 per FY: TCS rate is 20% on the incremental amount.
- TCS paid can be adjusted against advance tax or claimed as a refund during annual Income Tax Return filing, but represents an upfront cash outflow impact on portfolio rebalancing.

### 3. Monitoring & Aggregate Reporting
- Authorised Dealer (AD) banks must enforce real-time aggregation across all branches before executing outbound wire transfers.
