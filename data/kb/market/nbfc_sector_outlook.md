# Market Brief: Indian Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) Sector Outlook

**Date:** September 2026  
**Focus:** RBI Macroprudential Norms, Cost of Funds, and Credit Spreads

### 1. Regulatory Headwinds on NBFC Unsecured Lending
- The Reserve Bank of India has increased risk weights on unsecured consumer credit and bank lending to NBFCs, pushing cost of borrowings higher by 35–50 bps.
- Upper-tier NBFCs face compressed Net Interest Margins (NIMs) over the next 4–6 quarters.

### 2. High Concentration Exposure Risk
- Portfolios holding upwards of 25% single-sector concentration in NBFCs and retail lenders exhibit heightened downside beta during monetary tightening cycles.
- Wealth managers recommend trimming overweight domestic lending exposure and rebalancing into high-quality corporate debt or defensive global assets.
