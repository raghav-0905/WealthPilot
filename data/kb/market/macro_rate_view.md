# Macroeconomic Brief: Interest Rate Cycle & INR/USD Trajectory

**Date:** September 2026  
**Focus:** RBI MPC Policy, Global Central Banks, and Currency Impact

### 1. Domestic Monetary Policy
- The RBI Monetary Policy Committee maintains a policy repo rate of 6.50% with an ongoing stance of withdrawal of accommodation.
- Real interest rates in India are stable near 1.5–2.0%, supporting domestic fixed income and long-duration sovereign bonds.

### 2. Currency & External Sector (USD / INR)
- USD/INR volatility has compressed within a narrow trading range (83.20 - 84.10) backed by robust RBI forex reserves.
- However, persistent inflation differentials favor allocating 5-10% of high-net-worth wealth offshore to hedge against multi-year currency depreciation.
